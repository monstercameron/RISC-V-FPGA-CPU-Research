## Outliers worth noting

- **Window size:** Apple Firestorm (~630), Oryon (680), X925 (768 in flight) and Lion Cove (576) sit well above Zen 5 (448). Zen 5 reportedly runs out of integer registers before ROB entries.
- **Front end without a uop cache:** Skymont (3x3 decode clusters), Apple and Arm X4 deliver 8-10 wide without one. The cache is a power and fixed-width-decode trick, less useful for fixed 32-bit RISC-V.
- **Taken branches per cycle:** Lion Cove and Skymont handle 1-2; Zen 5 predicts two ahead; our core fetches 32 wide but the useful width ends at the first taken branch.
- **L1 BTB size:** Zen 5 16K, Oryon 2048, Apple 1024-class, Lion Cove ~2 KB-of-code zero bubble. Ours is 1K entries.
- **Instruction cache:** Apple and Oryon 192 KB 6-way, Lion Cove 64 KB, X925 64 KB 4-way. All set associative with 64 B lines; ours is direct mapped with 8 B lines.
- **Memory forwarding:** Lion Cove zero-latency exact-match forwarding at 2/cycle; Zen 2+ memory file; Skymont 2 cycles. Ours already has memory renaming.
- **Data hierarchy:** Lion Cove's three data levels (48 KB 4c, 192 KB 9c, 2.5 MB) show a small fast L0 with a large slower L1 is now mainstream.
- **Predicted-to-be-wrong claims to check:** Apple ROB counting differs by source (630 uops vs 330 groups).
