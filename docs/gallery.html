La Galerie

67 portraits of a processor

Each drawing is generated from the architecture history: one canvas per version, its units sized and arranged by their recorded counts. Walk the rooms in order and the machine learns to fetch, to pair, to pipeline, to run out of order. Select a work to see it enlarged.

fetch, loads rename, reorder buffer, stores execution, caches registers, multiply retirement, prediction

Salle I

Les Premiers Pas

One instruction at a time: a multicycle core finds its memory.

Salle II

Le Duo et le Trio

Two, then three instructions in the same clock.

Salle III

La Chaîne

The same contract, drawn again as a pipeline.

Salle IV

Le Désordre

Out of order: instructions run when ready, retire in order.

Salle V

L'Octuple

Eight wide, and learning to guess.

Salle VI

Le Grand Format

Sixteen wide, and then a council of engineers.

Salle VII

Le Conseil

The council widens everything at once: doors, room and breath, then a mending, a deeper memory for returns, and a fourth hand for branches.

Salle VIII

L'Archive

The council turns to a second measure, the LZMA kernel of 7-Zip: a quicker multiply, honest offsets, a mended way home, floors of memory below, and a predictor with a long memory.

Salle IX

Les Lettres en Vol

Mendings for the branch buffer, a near memory and a ledger of waiting loads, six tables of long memory, then several reads in flight at once, a weld that spans two clocks, and loads that sleep until their answer comes.

Salle X

La Virgule Flottante

Numbers that float: single and double precision arrive in the out-of-order core, built in only on request, then add, multiply, divide and root at the latencies of Lion Cove and Zen 5.

Salle XI

Les Vecteurs

Vectors as an option: a first subset of the RISC-V vector extension, executed in order at the head of the reorder buffer, with the rest of the instructions still to come.

Salle XII

Les Lignes

Wider lines for the data cache, as an option: a miss fills a longer line, answered one beat at a time, the critical beat first.

Salle XIII

Le Présage

A guess for every indirect jump, as an option: the target of a jump that is not a return is predicted at fetch from tagged tables that remember the path taken.
