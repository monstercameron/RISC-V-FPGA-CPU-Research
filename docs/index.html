The shape of a processor, one version at a time

An original 64-bit RISC-V core for FPGAs, built in public. Here its architecture is hung as a gallery: 29 drawings, from a core that fetched one instruction at a time to one that renames, reorders and retires sixteen, and a notebook that keeps the dated record of how it got there.

III.

The method

The target is RVA23U64, in stages. The current design implements RV64I with M, Zba, Zbb and Zbs, in three implementations of one contract. Results are simulated, labelled as such, and never presented as hardware.

Newest on the wall

No. 29, Les Seize Voix

Version 0.12.0, 21 novembre 2026 (in progress, not yet tagged). Sixteen instructions a clock through fetch, rename and retirement, before a window twice the size: 256 in the reorder buffer, 288 physical registers, 128 waiting. Behind it the same eight execution units, unchanged.
